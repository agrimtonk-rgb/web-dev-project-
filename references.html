Note on Data Collection

The data presented in Urja Setu is collected directly from village communities experiencing power outages. All statistics and reports are based on user-submitted information and reflect real experiences of rural electricity consumers. For academic or official use, we recommend cross-referencing with government databases and regional electricity board records.
