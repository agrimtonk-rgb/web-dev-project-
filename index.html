The Problem

Villages across India face frequent and unpredictable power cuts that disrupt daily life, education, farming activities, healthcare services, and small businesses. These outages cause financial losses, educational setbacks, and compromise community safety. Without proper documentation and reporting, authorities remain unaware of the severity and frequency of these issues, making it difficult to prioritize solutions.
