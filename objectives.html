Our Objectives

Urja Setu aims to create a comprehensive system for tracking, analyzing, and reporting electricity outages in rural areas, empowering communities to demand reliable power supply.

✔ Track Frequent Outages

Maintain a comprehensive record of all power cuts in villages, including dates, times, and durations to identify patterns.

✔ User-Friendly Data Collection

Provide a simple, accessible interface that anyone in the village can use to report outages, regardless of technical expertise.

✔ Auto-Generate Reports

Create professional, data-backed reports automatically that can be submitted to electricity boards and government authorities.

✔ Highlight Villager Problems

Document the real-world impact of outages on education, farming, health, safety, and business to show the human cost of power cuts.

✔ Enable Faster Response

Help authorities understand the severity and frequency of outages so they can prioritize infrastructure improvements and respond effectively.

✔ Community Empowerment

Give villages a voice and data-driven evidence to advocate for better electricity services and infrastructure development.

Making an Impact Together

By systematically documenting power outages and their effects, we create evidence that cannot be ignored. This data helps authorities allocate resources more effectively, prioritize infrastructure development, and ultimately improve the quality of life for thousands of rural households.
