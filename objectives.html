Our Objectives

Urja Setu is designed to make electricity outage reporting simple, transparent, and accessible for rural communities. It helps track power cuts, understand their impact, and create data-driven evidence for better electricity services.

✔ Track Frequent Outages

Record outage dates, timings, duration, and frequency to identify recurring power supply problems in villages.

✔ User-Friendly Data Collection

Provide a simple and accessible interface that allows villagers to report electricity outages easily, without requiring technical knowledge.

✔ Auto-Generate Reports

Automatically generate clear and professional reports from collected outage data to help authorities understand local power supply issues.

✔ Highlight Villager Problems

Capture the impact of frequent power cuts on education, farming, healthcare, safety, and small businesses in rural communities.

✔ Enable Faster Response

Provide accurate outage information that helps authorities identify critical areas, prioritize issues, and respond more effectively.

✔ Community Empowerment

Empower rural communities with reliable outage data to communicate their needs and advocate for better electricity services.

Making an Impact Together

By systematically documenting power outages and their effects, Urja Setu creates reliable evidence for meaningful action. This information helps authorities identify critical areas, improve resource allocation, prioritize infrastructure development, and support better electricity services for rural communities.
